How should color work across the brand?

Constraints: no blue (oversaturated in healthcare), no pink (gender-specific, MSO conflict). Viable hues: orange, emerald green, purple. Dark slate confirmed as neutral complement. Service lines use teal, green, orange, and purple.

Considerations

Option A: One Color

  • Simplest to manage — one color across everything
  • Strong, unified brand recognition
  • Service type communicated by name only, not visually
  • Harder to differentiate at a glance as locations grow

Option B: 4 Colors (Original)

  • Uses original brand blueprint colors (teal replaces blue)
  • Softer, more grounded feel
  • Master brand stays understated — service lines shine
  • Direct comparison point for the warm palette

Option C: 4 Colors (Warm)

  • Service types are instantly recognizable by color
  • Master brand stays understated — service lines shine
  • The homepage is a neutral "directory" framing
  • Warmer, richer hues with more depth
